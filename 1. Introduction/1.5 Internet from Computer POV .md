## Internet from Computer POV

So basically, if you went on youtube.com and click a video, your computer creates a packet and sends this packet to youtube.com over the internet. The packet will firstly be sent to home router and then the home router sends the packet to youtube.com. And we can think that this packet is the user request message to the youtube.com. This request message gives information to youtube.com about the video you want to watch. And after youtube.com receives your request message about watching specific video, its naturally realize that you want to watch video hence YT send the related video to you over the internet.
