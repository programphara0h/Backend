## Internet from Computer POV

So basically, if you went on youtube.com and click a video, your computer creates a packet and sends this packet to youtube.com over the internet. The packet will firstly be sent to home router and then the home router sends the packet to youtube.com. And we can think that this packet is the user request message to the youtube.com. This request message gives information to youtube.com about the video you want to watch. And after youtube.com receives your request message about watching specific video, its naturally realize that you want to watch video hence YT send the related video to you over the internet.

However, there are important note on this, while you are watching any videos on YT, it sends the video to you piece by piece. This is known as **Streaming**. And with this streaming technology, user can watch the video without the need to download the whole video. In addition, the streaming transmission time varies depending on the user's internet speed. Hence basically, the packet transmission is actually the basis of connecting to the internet or communicating with a computer on the other side of the world. In addition to this, when we enter say YT.com, we communicate with a powerful computer which belongs to YT itself, and we call this powerful computer as a **Server**.
