  ## What does the Internet represent


In this world today there are many routers on the internet. And there is a special reason for this. So, at this moment, I would like to give you another piece of information about a router. A router as we all know is a device used to connect to the internet via ISP cable. On the other hand, router is also used to communicate with another computer on the other side of the world.


Router is also used for LANs all around the world to connect to each other. But in todays world, most of the people use a home-router whereas it is a combo device that is a mixture of a router and switch. This is because most home-routers nowadays also have an Access Point (AP) feature. In this way, it is possible for you to choose to use wired or wireless connection for you PC to the router. But Home router is only enough for small environment and few devices.


but back to the original question, why do we need a lot of routers instead of just 1 big router? Imagine there is only 1 router instead of millions distributed across the world, so all the devices need to be connected to that router and said router need to have millions of ports which is not feasible. But there is wireless connection, right? Literally yes but technically NO. Esp in Computer Science. This is because in CS discipline, if we give the entire load to a single point, we call this problem as the **'Single Point of Failure.'** An example scenario, if the 1 router used by millions of devices broke down, the entire world will crash at the same time. Another major problem is that the cable length because for LAN connection will need the longest cable in the world to stay connected.

---
#### Load Balancing

Load balancing solves the single point of failure problem like the above. Say if 30 routers broke down out of 1 million routers, the internet still continues to work properly, and nothing came crashing down. Only the affected network efficiency was reduced a bit because of router breakdown.
